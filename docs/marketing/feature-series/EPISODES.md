# Feature series — episode beats

## Episode 1 — Role in, questions out

**Hook (0–3s):** "You know the job. You don't know the questions."  
**Payoff (last 5s):** "InterviewOS builds a full set from your role and JD."

| Beat | Duration | Action on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold open | 3s | Landing `#how-it-works` or dashboard empty state |
| Setup | 15s | `/interviews/new`: role, level, interview type, paste JD snippet |
| Generate | 12s | Progress rail / generate CTA; cut to question list populating |
| Context card | 10s | Expand one question — why it matters + suggested answer peek |
| CTA | 5s | "Create your set" → signup overlay or end card |

**VO line:** "Enter the role you're interviewing for. InterviewOS generates realistic questions grounded in your job description — not a generic list."

---

## Episode 2 — Feedback that lands

**Hook:** "Generic prep won't tell you what's missing."  
**Payoff:** Score + strengths/gaps on a real answer.

| Beat | Duration | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open on workspace | 5s | Question selected, answer field |
| Type / paste answer | 12s | Submit; loading state |
| Feedback reveal | 20s | Score, strengths, improvements, follow-up chip |
| Compare | 10s | Toggle suggested answer or attempt history |
| CTA | 5s | "Practice the next question" |

**VO line:** "Each answer is scored against your target role — what worked, what's thin, and what to say differently."

---

## Episode 3 — Practice aloud

**Hook:** "The real interview isn't multiple choice."  
**Payoff:** Mic live, AI asks a follow-up.

| Beat | Duration | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | 5s | Workspace → "Practice aloud" |
| Mic check | 8s | Permission UI (blur email if needed) |
| Q&A loop | 25s | AI speaks question; candidate answers; one follow-up |
| Privacy beat | 8s | On-screen note: transcript saved, audio not uploaded |
| CTA | 5s | "End session" tease for episode 4 |

**VO line:** "Run a spoken mock in your browser. The AI interviewer listens, asks follow-ups, and saves a transcript for scoring — not your audio."

---

## Episode 4 — Your debrief, your history

**Hook:** "You need a scorecard, not a vibe check."  
**Payoff:** Six dimensions + history list.

| Beat | Duration | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| End mock | 5s | Complete session |
| Debrief load | 15s | Overall score + dimension breakdown scroll |
| Highlights | 15s | Strongest / weakest answer callouts |
| History | 12s | `/history` mock list; open prior debrief |
| CTA | 8s | Home or signup |

**VO line:** "Get a six-dimension debrief after every mock — strengths, gaps, and what to practice next. Everything stays in your private history."
